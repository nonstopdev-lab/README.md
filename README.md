## How we get big things done

1. **Scope it.** A short call, then a written plan with a fixed outcome and a clear price.
2. **Ship early.** A working version in front of you fast, not a big reveal at the end.
3. **Automate the boring parts.** Releases, reports and follow-ups run on their own.
4. **Grow it.** SEO, ads and content so the product gets found, not just built.
5. **Stay with it.** Updates, fixes and support after launch.
